BizTalk Consultant

As part of Master Data team for one of our customers in Europe you will be supporting a Middleware Biztalk application, and a custom Azure .NET Cloud Application, called Market Framework Processor.

Responsibilities:

  • Analyze architecture designs together with the architect, contribute to architecture documents and propose the right solution.
  • Provide technical support and design enhancement for various applications.
  • Reduce the need for manual operative interactions within the environment.
  • Evaluate designs and ensure compliance to business requirements.
  • Coordinate with customers and analyze all user requirements.
  • Write and update, alone or together with a dedicated analyst, functional and technical designs.
  • Analyze applications and develop the required modifications to the application.
  • Design and develop new web services or redesign/ extend existing ones.
  • Develop and implement various tests such as unit tests for software application.
  • Support analysts and Business in end to end and user acceptance testing.
  • Monitor design requirements and provide necessary support.
  • Maintain a high development quality by following development guidelines.
  • Work closely within our "agile" environment with Product Owner, Scrum Master, the Development team (Tech Lead, developers and testers) and business proxies.

Qualifications:

  • Minimum two years of relevant work experience with .NET (C#) is required.
  • Experience with ASP.NET MVC / Angular /WCF / Web API / MSMQ / HTML5 / CSS3 / JavaScript frameworks / MS SQL Server is required.
  • Knowledge of Single-Sign-On (SSO) concepts and usage of identity server is required.
  • Experience with Agile and Scrum is mandatory.
  • Professional experience with GIT / Jira is needed.
  • Professional experience with MS Biztalk is needed.
  • Knowledge of design principles such as:
  • Application Life-cycle Management
  • Object-relational mapping
  • Behavior Driven Development/Test Driven Development
  • SOLID: first five object-oriented design (OOD) principles
  • Practical experience with pair programming, code reviews and relative estimations in Story Points is an advantage.
  • Experience with CQRS/ES, Distributed Architectures, Azure Cloud or other cloud providers is a plus.
  • Knowledge of the Energy Market and MIG6 is a big plus.

Required Personal Skills

  • You can work independently and, in a team, and take ownership.
  • Attributes of great importance are openness, personal engagement, etc.
  • You prefer to work in an Agile manner and are resistant to stress.
  • Excellent working level of English (both written and verbal) is a must.
